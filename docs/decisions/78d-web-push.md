# Item 78d: "Your Mac needs you", a push with no content

| Date | Decision | Why | By | Undo by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 27 Nov 2026 | The Mac's consent sheet is not changed (its version stays 1); the phone's switch says, where it is turned on, that the relay keeps this phone's push address and that the notification says "Your Mac needs you" and nothing more | Only a person who turns the switch on, on the phone, gives the relay a push address, so that is where it is said; raising the Mac's sheet's version would ask everyone again for something most never turn on. The sheet's words are the owner's to change | Claude | Add a line to `rm.consent.relaySees` and raise `CONSENT_VERSION` in `remote/state.ts` |
| 27 Nov 2026 | The relay takes push addresses on exactly three hosts: `web.push.apple.com`, `fcm.googleapis.com`, `updates.push.services.mozilla.com`; the phone, the Mac and the relay check one rule (`pushAddressOk` in `@manut/remote-channel`, `webPushAddress` in the API, held equal by a test) | The design's "only known push services". Apple's page asks firewalls to allow `*.push.apple.com`, but Safari's subscriptions are on `web.push.apple.com`; Edge's push service (WNS) is left out, so Edge on a phone gets "use Safari or Chrome" | Claude | Add a host to `PUSH_HOSTS` and `WEB_PUSH_HOSTS` together |
| 27 Nov 2026 | The push key is two Wrangler secrets, `WEB_PUSH_PRIVATE_KEY` (base64url of the 32-byte scalar) and `WEB_PUSH_PUBLIC_KEY` (base64url of the 65-byte point), the format the web-push tools print; the JWT's `sub` is `PUBLIC_API_ORIGIN` | Both put with one `wrangler secret bulk` so neither half is echoed and no config file changes; the public half is offered to phones anyway. An https `sub` names us without an email address | Claude | Make `WEB_PUSH_PUBLIC_KEY` a var in `wrangler.jsonc` (the code reads either) |
| 27 Nov 2026 | The push key is offered in the public health answer (`webPush`), only while the relay is on and both halves are set | The web app already reads its relay switch there (`live.ts`), and a public key is public by design | Claude | Serve it from its own route |
| 27 Nov 2026 | The Mac keeps push addresses in local storage (`manut.remote.push`), not the keychain | An address is no secret: a push to it needs the relay's signed VAPID key. The keychain item's every field is checked by the shell, and a new shell command would be one more a script in the page could call | Claude | Move it into the `remote` keychain item in `remote_keys.rs` |
| 27 Nov 2026 | The Mac pushes when an ask opens that was not open before and no paired phone has a session open by either road; at most once an ask, never twice within two minutes, never for asks open when the switch turns on; an ask opening inside the gap is not pushed for later | The design's "when an ask opens and no phone is connected". The notification from less than two minutes ago still stands, and repeated buzzing for one stuck agent is worse than one | Claude | `PUSH_GAP_MS` and `PushPlan` in `remote/push.ts` |
| 27 Nov 2026 | The relay allows 30 `push` messages a clock hour per Mac, counted only for a push that reaches at least one address | Above the most an honest Mac sends (one each two minutes), so it stops only a Mac whose key is in the wrong hands | Claude | `RelayObject.pushesPerHour` |
| 27 Nov 2026 | Each push: no body, `TTL` 3600, `Urgency: high`, `Topic: manut-mac`; the JWT lasts 12 hours and is reused, in the isolate's memory only, until an hour is left | No payload means no encryption and nothing for the push service to see; an hour-old "needs you" is still worth hearing, a day-old one is not; Apple asks for a new token no more than hourly | Claude | `web-push.ts` |
| 27 Nov 2026 | A 404 or 410 forgets the address at the relay, and the relay tells the Mac with its own `webpush` and no address | Without the message the Mac would hand the dead address back at every `ready` | Claude | Drop the message in `relay-object.ts` and `link.ts` |
| 27 Nov 2026 | The phone sends its push address to the Mac as a seventh request inside the channel, `notify`, with no passkey; at every session it sends its address as it is now, or none | It acts on nothing of the Mac's and reaches no one but the phone that asked; sending it each session catches a Mac that missed a change and a browser that renewed its subscription, with no work in the service worker | Claude | `#notify` in `serve.ts`, `#syncNotify` in `web/src/app/mac/remote.ts` |
| 27 Nov 2026 | The relay does not skip a device whose phone has a relay socket open; the Mac decides who is connected | The Mac sees both roads (relay and tailnet); the relay sees one | Claude | Filter `targets` by `livePhones()` in `relay-object.ts` |
